
# Environment Setup

Having the right tools is crucial. Here's what I use:

-   **OS:** A Linux distribution (like Arch or Debian) is highly recommended.
-   **Debugger:** GDB with an extension like Pwndbg or GEF.
-   **Disassembler:** IDA Pro or Ghidra.
-   **Editor:** VS Code or Neovim with appropriate plugins.
